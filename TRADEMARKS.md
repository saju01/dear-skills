# Names and branding

The code in this repository is licensed under Apache-2.0. The licence covers the code, not the names: section 6 of Apache-2.0 grants no rights to use the project's names except to describe where the work comes from.

The project names are **dear-skills**, **dear-inbox-zero** and the **dear-** naming series. They identify the original project maintained at https://github.com/saju01/dear-skills.

## You can, without asking

- Say your work is "based on dear-inbox-zero", "a fork of dear-skills", or "works with dear-inbox-zero".
- Use the names in articles, tutorials, talks, reviews and comparisons.
- Redistribute **unmodified** releases under their original names.

## Please ask first (open an issue)

- Naming a modified version, fork or product with "dear-" or "dear-inbox-zero" in the main position (e.g. "dear-inbox-zero Pro").
- Using the names in a domain name, package name or social account.
- Anything that suggests your version is official, certified or endorsed by this project.

## Forks

Forks are welcome. Please give a modified version its own name and keep a line such as "based on dear-inbox-zero by saju01" with a link to this repository.

## Third-party names

"Inbox zero" is used in its common, descriptive sense (an empty inbox). This project is not affiliated with any company or product of that name. Microsoft, Outlook, Teams and Microsoft 365 are trademarks of the Microsoft group of companies, used here only to say what the skill works with.
